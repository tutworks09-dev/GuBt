1. Overview

GuBt Game Center ("we", "our", or "the website") provides browser-based games and related features. We aim to handle information responsibly and explain our practices clearly.

We do not currently use analytics tools or display advertisements on the website, based on the information available when this policy was prepared.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the games and features you use, information may be processed or stored by our server or database. The exact information may vary depending on the feature.

This may include information you submit or game-related data needed to provide a requested feature.

Web hosting and database providers may also process technical information as part of delivering and securing the website, according to their own practices.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Provide game features you choose to use.
  • Save or retrieve game-related information.
  • Find and fix technical problems.
  • Protect the service against misuse.

We do not claim to use information for analytics or targeted advertising.

4. Storage and Security

Some information may be stored on a server or in a database rather than only on your device.

The length of time information is retained depends on the feature and the service configuration.

Reasonable security measures should be used to protect stored information. However, no website or online transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If a feature provides account, save, or data-management controls, you can use those controls where available.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by hosting, database, or technical service providers when necessary to operate the website. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service and its users.

The exact providers depend on the services configured for the website.

6. Third-Party Services

The website may rely on hosting, database, or other technical services. These providers may have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

If you follow a link to another website, that website's privacy policy applies to its own services. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites.

7. Children's Privacy

GuBt Game Center is a games website that may be used by people of different ages.

We do not knowingly ask users to provide personal information unless a feature requires it. Please avoid submitting sensitive personal information through game features.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information, use an official contact method available on the website or its hosting platform, if one is available.

8. Your Choices

Only provide information needed for the feature you choose to use. You can stop using the website at any time.

If a feature provides account or data-management controls, you may use them to review or change information associated with that feature.

We do not currently list a dedicated privacy contact email, so this policy does not promise an email-based request process.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website's features, service providers, or data practices change.

When we publish an update, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

GuBt Game Center does not currently list a dedicated privacy contact email.

If you need to contact the website operator, use an official contact method provided on the website or by its hosting platform, if available.
